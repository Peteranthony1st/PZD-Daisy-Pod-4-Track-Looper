Grains presets live here (PRES001.DAT, PRES002.DAT, ...) -- each one is
audio-inclusive (the captured sample itself, plus every parameter), so
loading a preset restores the exact sound, not just settings.

PRES001-009 ship pre-loaded as starter presets (bell/water shimmer,
ethereal piano, guitar loop, atonal soundscape, synthetic crickets, bass
drone, ice-rain glitch, water bubbles, sea/bells) -- all CC0-licensed
source audio from freesound.org (bassimat), 5-second excerpts, engine
default parameters. Delete or overwrite any of them like any other
preset; your own saves just continue from PRES010 onward.
